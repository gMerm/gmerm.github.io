Georgios Mermigkis

Ph.D. Student · CEID, University of Patras

Explainable AI Language Models High-Performance Computing ML for Metamaterials Reliable Edge AI

I am a Ph.D. student in the High-Performance Computing Group at the Department of Computer Engineering & Informatics (CEID), University of Patras, advised by Assoc. Prof. Panagiotis Hadjidoukas. I am also a collaborating researcher at the Industrial Systems Institute, Athena Research Center, and a research affiliate of the Applied Computational Engineering Lab at New York University Abu Dhabi.

My research spans machine learning, explainable artificial intelligence, language models and high-performance computing. I work on making AI explanations accurate and accessible, on the reliability of LLMs and vision models, on explainable ML for architected materials, and on scaling scientific workloads such as tera-scale genomic PCA.

I received my Integrated Master's Degree (Diploma) from CEID, University of Patras (2020–2025). Alongside research, I maintain the department's HPC systems as a Computing Center Technician, and I previously worked as a software engineer at VectorCamp on high-performance software and LLMs.
