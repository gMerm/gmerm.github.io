01About

I am a Ph.D. student in the HPC Group at CEID, University of Patras, advised by Assoc. Prof. Panagiotis Hadjidoukas, a collaborating researcher at the Industrial Systems Institute (Athena RC), and a research affiliate of the ECM Lab at NYU Abu Dhabi.

My research spans machine learning, explainable artificial intelligence, language models and high-performance computing. I work on making AI explanations verified and accessible, including how well LLMs adapt their explanations to different audiences, and on agentic XAI frameworks. I also study explainability-guided token pruning in VLMs, the reliability of LLMs and VLMs, explainable ML for architected materials, and scaling scientific workloads such as tera-scale genomic PCA.

I received my Integrated Master's Degree (Diploma) from CEID, University of Patras (2020–2025). Alongside research, I maintain the department's HPC systems as a Computing Center Technician, and I previously worked as a software engineer at VectorCamp on high-performance software and LLMs.
